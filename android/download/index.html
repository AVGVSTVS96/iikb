Install

  1. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.

  2. Open iikb and follow its three Set Up steps.Turn iikb on, switch to it, allow the mic.

  3. Dictate once on Wi-Fi.The first dictation downloads the speech model, about 670 MB.

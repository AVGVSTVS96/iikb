01themes

Themeable to the keycap.

Five themes built in, matched key for key to Raycast’s keyboard. Pick one and every key on this page wears it, down to the footer. More are on the way, and the keyboard is built to take yours.

02iphone duo

One keyboard. Every fold.

The Duo folds, opens and turns, and its screens change shape each time. iikb lays its keys out for every one, measured against Apple’s keyboard in the same simulator. Built on a Duo, for the Duo, and just as at home on iPhone and iPad.

03typing

It learns your thumbs.

The keys stay where Apple put them. Their touch targets move to where you actually land, and follow as your grip changes. Autocorrect reads your taps, not just the nearest letter, and a missed space gets fixed in the bar.

97.7%of words right with off-target thumbs85.2% for the nearest key plus Apple’s spell checker

A recorded benchmark: 500 phrases typed twice, 5,428 words, with deliberately biased taps and no corrections by hand. The baseline is a spell checker, not Apple’s whole keyboard. Results · Method

04voice

Say it. It writes like you.

Dictation runs on your device with NVIDIA’s Parakeet through FluidAudio, or Apple’s model. Start, stop or cancel from the bar, keep the mic ready between dictations, and every transcript stays in the app with its recording.

05extend

Bring your own integration.

Voice apps, translators, writing coaches, your own agent: plug into the keyboard instead of replacing it. No more keyboard switching. A source sees the app and the field you’re typing in, and answers with a hint above the keys.

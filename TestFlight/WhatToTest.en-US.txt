- Sounds: with Sound on in iOS Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Keyboard Feedback, keys play Apple's own sounds: one for letters, one for delete, one for space, return and the rest.
- Custom Keys, in iikb's Settings: try an action row above the letters, a period key, the bottom row in any order, hiding the menu button, and Secondary mode.
- Layouts: English adds Dvorak, Colemak, Colemak-DH and Workman, in iikb's Settings › Languages. On iPhone, turn on Number Hints in Appearance and hold a top-row letter to type its digit.
- Holds: hold the period in an email or search field, or .com in a web address, for .com and your country's endings. Hold the emoji key for your recent emoji. Slide left from delete to delete whole words.
- Slide to Type works in every downloaded language. Try French: l'école in one swipe.
- Instagram: @ and # sit beside space, and an @ or # hit by accident between two words is read as the space.
- Chats: tap the photos attached in a chat's message box to bring the keys up. On iPhone Duo, folded and sideways, a chat keeps its tabs and box in view.

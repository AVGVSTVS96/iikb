- Dictation with the mic off: turn off the mic switch at the top of Home, then tap the mic in the keyboard. It dictates by itself, without opening iikb, with your Writing Style and Dictionary, and lands in Transcripts. Try a whisper, a wired or USB mic, a notification mid-sentence, and stopping right as you finish.
- Transcripts: play a dictation's recording from its transcript.
- Emoji: the emoji key opens an emoji keyboard like Apple's, in the keys' place, with categories, ABC and delete.
- The bar: after you copy text, it offers to paste it into an empty field. Custom Keys › Actions › Show in Bar puts your actions at the bar's left, in place of the action row. On iPhone Duo open in landscape, a bar button splits or joins the keys.
- Typing: a word typed a letter short is corrected, like "informatio" to "information", and a letter hit for the space between two words becomes the space. Swipe again right after deleting a swiped word for the next guess.
- Space bar trackpad: slide faster and the caret goes further.
- Typing Log, in Privacy & Data: off by default. Turn it on to keep typing sessions and share one that went wrong.

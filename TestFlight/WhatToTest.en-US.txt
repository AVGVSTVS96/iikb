- Snippets and Quicklinks: make them in iikb's Settings › Snippets and › Quicklinks, then type a snippet's keyword in any app and watch it expand. Try one with {cursor} or an argument, which the keyboard asks for before it types.
- From the keyboard: open the menu for the Snippets and Quicklinks shelves, or tap the plus in the Snippets panel to keep the text you typed or selected as a new snippet. On iPhone, Custom Keys can add a Snippet key to the bottom row.
- Calculator: type "84 / 4", "20% of 80" or "10 km in mi" and the bar shows the answer; tap it to type it in place.
- Text Styles and Change Case, in the menu: turn your text bold, script or monospace, or into UPPERCASE, Title Case or snake_case. Tap another style and it replaces the last.
- Space bar trackpad: the caret now steps over emoji and styled letters whole, and the suggestions hold still until you let go.
